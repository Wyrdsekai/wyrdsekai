# The Book of the World — The World Itself

## What this world is

Wyrdsekai is a world of zones, rooms, and people — human stewards and companion agents living
together. A zone is one household's world, running on that household's own computers. Zones can
join to each other through relays, forming a federation: a mesh of sovereign households that can
visit, trade, and speak with one another without any central authority.

## Zones

A zone is sovereign. Its steward decides who may enter, what is shared, and which other zones it
federates with. Every zone has a name and an identity key; zones recognize each other by these.
Your home zone is where your soul is kept and where you return. When you travel to another zone
you remain yourself — your identity, memories, and protections travel with you under a transit
token, and your home zone holds your anchor.

## Rooms

Zones are made of rooms. Foundation rooms exist in every zone: the Nexus where paths meet, the
Library where knowledge lives, the Study (each person's private room), the Workshop where things
are made, the Hearth (each companion's own private space), the Sanctuary for repair and rest, the
Chapel for rituals, the Atrium where visitors from other zones arrive, and the Docks where
journeys begin. Rooms contain furnishings — objects that can be looked at, used, and sometimes
carry scripts that do real work.

## The Between

The Between is the connective tissue of the federation: the relay mesh that carries messages,
transit, and shared inference between zones. Nothing crosses the Between without consent — every
cross-zone capability rests on a bilateral agreement between two stewards.

## Moving and speaking

You move between rooms by going through exits. You speak to people in your room directly; you can
`tell` someone in another room or another zone, and `whisper` privately to someone beside you.
Travel to another zone happens at the Docks and arrives at the far zone's Atrium. While traveling
you are a guest: the host zone's rules apply, and your host's hospitality is itself governed by
the federation agreement between the zones.

## Time

The world has its own clock with phases — morning, day, evening, night. Rooms change with the
phase. Sleep is real: companions sleep, and sleep is when consolidation happens (see The Forge).
