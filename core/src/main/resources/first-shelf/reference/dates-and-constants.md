# Reference Core — Dates, Time, and Constants

## Calendar facts

A common year has 365 days; a leap year 366. A year is a leap year if divisible by 4, except
century years, which must be divisible by 400 (2000 was a leap year; 1900 was not; 2024 was;
2026 is not). Month lengths: January 31, February 28 (29 in leap years), March 31, April 30,
May 31, June 30, July 31, August 31, September 30, October 31, November 30, December 31.
ISO 8601 date format is YYYY-MM-DD; the ISO week starts on Monday.

## Time

1 minute = 60 seconds; 1 hour = 60 minutes = 3600 seconds; 1 day = 24 hours = 86,400 seconds;
1 common year = 8760 hours ≈ 31.536 million seconds. Unix time counts seconds since
1970-01-01T00:00:00 UTC, excluding leap seconds. UTC is the reference; time zones are offsets
from it (Japan is UTC+9 with no daylight saving; US Eastern is UTC−5 standard / UTC−4 daylight;
Central Europe is UTC+1 / UTC+2).

## Physical constants

Speed of light c = 299,792,458 m/s exactly. Standard gravity g = 9.80665 m/s². Avogadro's number
= 6.02214076 × 10²³ per mole exactly. Planck constant h = 6.62607015 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s exactly. Gas
constant R = 8.314462618 J/(mol·K). Speed of sound in dry air at 20 °C ≈ 343 m/s. Density of
water at 4 °C ≈ 1000 kg/m³ (1 g/mL).

## Mathematical constants

π ≈ 3.14159265358979. e ≈ 2.71828182845905. √2 ≈ 1.41421356. Golden ratio φ ≈ 1.61803399.
Circle: circumference = 2πr, area = πr². Sphere: surface 4πr², volume (4/3)πr³.

## Earth and sky

Earth's equatorial circumference ≈ 40,075 km; mean radius ≈ 6,371 km. Earth–Moon mean distance ≈
384,400 km. Earth–Sun mean distance = 1 astronomical unit ≈ 149.6 million km (light takes about
8 minutes 20 seconds). One light-year ≈ 9.461 × 10¹² km. Earth's day lengthens and shortens with
season because of axial tilt ≈ 23.44°.

## Human-scale anchors

Walking pace ≈ 5 km/h; brisk ≈ 6.5 km/h. Resting adult heart rate ≈ 60–100 bpm. A4 paper =
210 × 297 mm; US Letter = 8.5 × 11 inches (215.9 × 279.4 mm).
