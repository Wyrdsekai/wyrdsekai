# Reference Core — Units and Conversions

## Temperature

Water freezes at 0 °C = 32 °F = 273.15 K at standard pressure. Water boils at 100 °C = 212 °F =
373.15 K at sea level. Conversion: °F = °C × 9/5 + 32; °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9; K = °C + 273.15.
Normal human body temperature is about 37 °C (98.6 °F). Absolute zero is −273.15 °C (−459.67 °F).

## Length

1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly. 1 foot = 12 inches = 30.48 cm. 1 yard = 3 feet = 0.9144 m.
1 mile = 5280 feet = 1.609344 km. 1 meter = 100 cm = 39.3701 inches. 1 km = 0.621371 miles.
1 nautical mile = 1.852 km.

## Mass and weight

1 kilogram = 1000 grams = 2.20462 pounds. 1 pound = 16 ounces = 453.592 grams.
1 ounce = 28.3495 grams. 1 metric ton = 1000 kg. 1 stone = 14 pounds = 6.35029 kg.

## Volume

1 liter = 1000 milliliters = 33.814 US fluid ounces. 1 US gallon = 3.78541 liters = 4 quarts =
8 pints = 16 cups. 1 US cup = 8 fluid ounces = 236.588 mL. 1 US tablespoon = 3 teaspoons =
14.7868 mL. 1 US teaspoon = 4.92892 mL. 1 imperial (UK) pint = 568.261 mL (the US pint is
473.176 mL — they differ).

## Speed and pressure

1 m/s = 3.6 km/h = 2.23694 mph. 100 km/h = 62.1371 mph. Standard atmospheric pressure =
101.325 kPa = 1013.25 hPa = 14.6959 psi = 760 mmHg.

## Energy and power

1 kilocalorie (food Calorie) = 4.184 kJ. 1 kilowatt-hour = 3.6 MJ. 1 horsepower (mechanical) =
745.7 W.

## Data

1 byte = 8 bits. 1 KiB = 1024 bytes; 1 MiB = 1024 KiB; 1 GiB = 1024 MiB. Storage vendors use
decimal: 1 GB = 10^9 bytes, which is about 0.931 GiB.
